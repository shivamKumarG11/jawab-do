Right to Information Act, 2005 · on Arkiv, the Web3 database

The government has 30 days to answer.
Now the clock is public.

Jawab Do tracks RTI deadlines as Arkiv entities: records that expire on their own schedule, a timeline nobody can quietly edit, and ownership that moves with the appeal.

–trusted filings
–deemed refused
–awaiting reply
–in appeal
⌛Entity Expiration

Each request is kept for its ordinary appeal window; timeline events for a year.

🔒Burn-owned timeline

Every event is readonly and handed to 0x…dEaD in the same batch. Even we can't edit it.

🪪$creator trust

Spoofs can copy every attribute, but not the creator. The scorecard counts trusted desks only.

⇄Ownership on appeal

Second appeal moves $owner to the legal-aid desk; the creator never changes.

Synthetic demo on the Arkiv Tiramisu testnet. No applicant names, contact details or application text are stored. Departments are generic types, not real offices. This app does not submit official RTIs or verify real-world filings.
