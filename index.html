Essential goods · India · on Arkiv, the Web3 database

Every batch of essentials,
one open record.

Where a packet of dal or a strip of medicine came from, who holds it now, when it expires, whether it was recalled, and whether its price is fair against today's official government price. No company owns the record, so no platform can wall it off.

🏭Origin can't be faked

Every batch carries the wallet that created it ($creator). A copied brand made by an unlicensed wallet is flagged counterfeit.

🤝Custody = ownership

Selling stock hands the batch record to the buyer ($owner). Split, sold and recalled steps are readonly and burned.

⌛Things expire on their own

A batch lives until its best-before date, an offer for 48 h, a price for 48 h, a licence for a year. Stale data disappears by itself.

🔓Open to every app

Any seller app, logistics firm or regulator reads the same records straight from Arkiv. Recalls arrive live over WebSocket.

Real: official daily retail prices from the Department of Consumer Affairs (fcainfoweb.nic.in). Synthetic: every company, licence, batch and trade is demo data on the Arkiv Tiramisu testnet. Not a real marketplace.

Today's official prices

createdBy(oracle) · expires in 48 h

All-India average retail prices published daily by the Department of Consumer Affairs, Price Monitoring Division. Our oracle posts them to Arkiv; each day's price expires by itself after 48 hours, so only current prices are ever shown.
